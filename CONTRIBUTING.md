## Introduction

Thank you for deciding to contribute to this project; your effort will provide the project with substantial benefit. It's crucial to have a contributing guide in order to share common ground.


Any kind of *contribution* is welcome here; however, it would be better if you decided to work on one of the features below.

- Encrypted storage of IPC credentials
- Enhanced cross-platform compatibility
- Compatibility with embedded network devices

## How to Contribute

To contribute to the project, you can follow these steps:

- Create your own *fork* of the code.
- Make the changes in your fork.
- *Ensure* that your commits **comply with** *Conventional Commits* standards.
- If you like the changes and think the project could benefit from them, *consider* sending a *pull request* that **clearly explains** *the changes* you have made.

## How to Report a Bug or an Issue

You can open an *issue* on the project's Issues page to take a step toward solving the problem. If the bug you have just discovered has **severe** *impacts* on the *security* of the users, please contact the project owner [here](mailto:dc-ipcs.essence009@passinbox.com) **first**.
